Upload clear photos of the beneficiary and relevant documents such as medical reports, school certificates, or ID proof. High-quality, authentic photos greatly increase donor trust and your fundraiser's chances of success.

Got questions?
Frequently Asked
Questions
🚀 How do I start a fundraiser? ▾
Click "Start a Fundraiser for Free", fill in your cause details, add photos and documents, and submit. Our team will review and approve your fundraiser within 24 hours. You'll get notified via email and SMS once it's live.
💸 Is there any platform fee? ▾
No! DonateTracker charges absolutely 0% platform fee. 100% of the donated amount goes directly to the fundraiser. We sustain the platform through optional tips from donors.
📄 How do I get my Donation Certificate? ▾
Every donor receives an official Donation Certificate after making a contribution. Simply enter your name and email during checkout — your certificate will be sent instantly once your donation is confirmed. Additionally, if you wish to claim tax benefits under Section 80G, provide your PAN at checkout and an 80G certificate will be sent within 3–5 working days.
🏦 How and when are funds transferred? ▾
Funds are transferred to your KYC-verified bank account. You can request a withdrawal anytime from your dashboard. Transfers are processed within 3–7 working days via NEFT/RTGS.
🔒 Is my donation safe and secure? ▾
Yes. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption. We use industry-standard payment gateways (Razorpay/PayU). Every fundraiser is verified by our team before going live to ensure authenticity.
❌ What if the fundraiser doesn't reach its goal? ▾
All funds raised — even if the goal isn't met — are transferred to the fundraiser. There's no "all-or-nothing" policy. Every rupee donated reaches the beneficiary.
